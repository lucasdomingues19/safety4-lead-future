## DESIGN PRINCIPLES TO FOLLOW

1. **Whitespace**: Use generous margins and padding for breathing room
2. **Color Hierarchy**: Primary blue for CTAs and highlights, navy for text, lime for accents
3. **Typography Hierarchy**: Large bold headlines, medium subheaders, regular body text
4. **Visual Rhythm**: Alternate between text-heavy and visual-heavy pages
5. **Icons/Emojis**: Use consistently (⚡🧠📈👥🎓🏅 etc.) to add visual interest without clutter
6. **Consistency**: Same spacing, border treatments, card styles throughout
7. **Data Focus**: Highlight statistics prominently with large numbers, secondary text smaller
8. **Modern Professional**: Clean lines, minimal shadows, flat design with subtle depth

---

## CONTENT NOTES

- **Copilot Course**: Should feel like the hero/flagship with premium treatment (golden badge, 2 full pages, larger visual emphasis)
- **Statistics**: Make them POP with large bold numbers, use primary blue color
- **Professional Tone**: Sophisticated, authoritative, but approachable
- **Visual Balance**: Avoid walls of text; break up with cards, boxes, icons
- **Brand Consistency**: Every page should feel connected through color, typography, spacing
